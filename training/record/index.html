Voice recording · Grabación de voz

For the bilingual tiny-voca voice. Everything stays on this device until you download it. · Todo se queda en este dispositivo hasta que lo descargues.

1. Before you start · Antes de empezar

Then record sentence 1 and look at its checks: aim for a level between −18 and −3 dB, never clipped. · Luego graba la frase 1 y revisa.

2. Record · Grabar

Read it once the way you would say it to a friend. Wait half a second before speaking and after finishing. To redo, just record again. · Léela como se la dirías a un amigo. Espera medio segundo antes y después.

3. Download · Descargar

One zip per session: WAV files (16-bit, mono, the microphone's own rate), metadata.csv and info.json. Download after each session, and keep a copy. · Un zip por sesión.
